# M9 — Start at login (macOS) — tester checklist

Machine: the friend's Mac (macOS 13 or later), CI-built DMG installed into
`/Applications`. Do not run from the DMG or from Downloads: those copies are
App-Translocated and the app refuses to register them (expected, see step 2).

Automated part: the plist and stale-path unit tests in
`native/crates/platform-macos/src/autostart.rs` run on the macOS CI runner
(`cargo test --workspace` on `macos-latest`); the ignored `real_home_round_trip`
test runs only with `cargo test -p platform-macos -- --ignored` on a Mac.

## Setup
1. Drag `ShareCtrlScreen.app` into `/Applications`, open it from there once.
2. Open **Settings** in the app. Turn **Start at login** on.
   - Expected: the toggle stays on. If the app was opened from the DMG or
     Downloads, the toggle turns back off with the message "...temporary copy
     macOS made for a downloaded app. Move it to Applications...". Fix: quit,
     move to Applications, reopen, retry.

## Plist on disk
3. In Terminal: `cat ~/Library/LaunchAgents/dev.netameta.sharectrl.plist`
   - Expected: `Label` is `dev.netameta.sharectrl`; `ProgramArguments` holds
     `/Applications/ShareCtrlScreen.app/Contents/MacOS/sharectrl` and then
     `--autostart`; `RunAtLoad` is `true`; there is no `KeepAlive`.
4. `ls ~/Library/LaunchAgents/` shows no leftover `*.plist.tmp`.

## System Settings
5. System Settings → **General → Login Items & Extensions**.
   - Expected: a notice about a background item, or ShareCtrlScreen listed under
     **Allow in the Background** with its toggle **on**.
   - If the toggle is off, sign-in will not start the app even though the plist
     exists. Turn it on there, then repeat step 6.

## Sign-in behaviour
6. Quit the app completely (menu bar or Cmd+Q). Log out, then log back in.
   - Expected: ShareCtrlScreen starts by itself, **minimized** (not in front).
     It is reachable for a remote connection without any click.
7. Turn **Start at login** off in Settings.
   - Expected: the plist is gone (`ls ~/Library/LaunchAgents/` no longer lists
     `dev.netameta.sharectrl.plist`). The running instance keeps running.
   - Log out and back in: the app does **not** start by itself.

## Stale path
8. With the toggle on, move the app to `~/Applications` (or rename the bundle),
   open it from the new location, quit.
   - Expected: `cat` on the plist now shows the new path; the toggle is on.
     (Start at login is re-pointed at every launch.)
9. Log out and back in: the app starts from the new location.

## Pass criteria
- Steps 2–9 behave as described.
- No error text other than the translocation message appears in Settings.
- `~/Library/Logs/ShareCtrlScreen/app.log` has "start at sign-in registered"
  and, after step 7, "start at sign-in removed".
